# Sweep, 2026-11-22 (computer only, 3.22.4)

A bug hunt on `main` at 3.22.4 (versionCode 59), run on a computer with Robolectric: no phone. The new test class is
`app/src/test/java/dev/qawalah/sunlighn/SweepTest.java` (49 tests). No app code was changed. One existing test was
corrected (see "Suite result").

**How to run**

```bash
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest
```

The tests that show a bug below are skipped unless asked for, so the suite stays green for releases. To see them fail:

```bash
SWEEP_KNOWN_BUGS=1 ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest --tests '*SweepTest*'
```

B2 freezes the app, and the test run with it. Run it on its own, with a time limit:

```bash
SWEEP_HANGS=1 timeout 150 ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest --tests '*SweepTest.savedAnyTimeShotOpens'
```

When a bug is fixed, delete the `knownBug("Bn")` (or `knownHang`) line at the top of its test. The test then guards
the fix.

## Bugs, most severe first

### B1 (high). Build the shot shows the previous answer while it works out the new one, and Stand here goes to it

- **Steps:** Build the shot → a subject → wait for the answer (Sunset, "Soonest · Today · 2:50 PM") → Settings →
  Light: Sunrise → Done → tap Fly here (or Stand here) before the new answer arrives. On a phone that is easy with
  Look in: Next year, or on a slower phone.
- **What happens:** the settings row says "Sunrise · …". The headline still says "Soonest · Today · 2:50 PM" and When
  says "Today to Nov 24": both are the Sunset answer. The picture still shows the afternoon path in the south-west.
  Fly here is enabled, and it opens Your shot at **2:50 PM**, a sunset, for a Sunrise request. "Working it out…" never
  shows once there has been an answer.
- **What should happen:** while a new answer is worked out, the old one is not shown as the answer: "Working it out…",
  with Stand here greyed (or Stand here waits for the new answer).
- **Most likely at fault:** `ComposeScreen.java`
  - `solve()` (line 991) sets `solving = true` but keeps the old `runs`.
  - `words()` (line 927) shows "Working it out…" only when `runs.isEmpty()`, so with old runs it draws the old answer
    and enables `stand`.
  - `go()` (line 1089) uses `runs` whether or not `solving`.
  - The same stale `runs` feed `rise()`, `distance()`, `paths()` and the Distance slider (`workOutDist`) until the new
    answer is in.
- **Test:** `standHereBeforeTheNewAnswerIsIn`. It holds the main thread so the worker's answer cannot be drawn, as on
  a slow phone.

### B2 (high on the flat map). Opening a shot with no time freezes the app

- **Steps:**
  - Home → Saved → tap a shot that says "Any time" (2.1's saved lines became such shots, 3.0).
  - Or open a shared link to a shot with no time ("any time it lines up").
- **What happens:** the main thread loops forever: no crash, no log, the app stops answering. Stack:

  ```
  org.osmdroid.views.Projection.getCloserPixel(Projection.java:500)
  org.osmdroid.views.Projection.getLongPixelFromMercator(Projection.java:531)
  org.osmdroid.views.Projection.toPixels(Projection.java:207)
  org.osmdroid.views.MapView.zoomToBoundingBox(MapView.java:567)
  dev.qawalah.sunlighn.OsmMap.fit(OsmMap.java:99)
  dev.qawalah.sunlighn.MainActivity.fit(MainActivity.java:1101)
  dev.qawalah.sunlighn.MainActivity.lambda$openShot (MainActivity.java:3112)
  ```

  A shot with no time opens on When (`openShot`, `MainActivity.java:3109`). The map is then squeezed under the sheet to
  **66 px** tall (measured). The fit pads by 40 dp (120 px) on each side, which is more than the map's height, and
  osmdroid's `zoomToBoundingBox` never returns. A shot with a time opens Your shot at half height and is fine.
- **What should happen:** When opens with the map in sight, and the fit never asks for more padding than the map has.
- **Most likely at fault:**
  - `OsmMap.fit` (`OsmMap.java:93-99`) guards against a map with no size, but not against one shorter than `2 × pad`.
  - `openShot` (`MainActivity.java:3109`) does not set the sheet's height for When as `goShot` does for Your shot. Why
    the sheet takes all but 66 px was not traced further.
- **Who it hits:** the flat map. That is Settings → 3D map: Off, phones without OpenGL ES 3 or an Arm chip, a failed 3D
  start, and every screen test. The 3D map (`LibreMap.fit`) hands the padding to MapLibre and probably does not hang,
  but it cannot be run on a computer. Check on the phone with 3D map Off, then On.
- **Tests:** `savedAnyTimeShotOpens`, `sharedLinkAnyTime` (`SWEEP_HANGS=1`).

### B3 (medium). Across the date line the map shows the whole world, not the line

- **Steps:** Line up a street with the subject and You on either side of 180° (for example Taveuni, Fiji: You at
  −16.8000, 179.9990, the subject at −16.8010, −179.9990, 220 m apart). Open When.
- **What happens:** the map zooms out to zoom 2, centred on longitude 0 (the Arctic and Africa), and neither pin nor the
  line is on screen. The times and the bearing (118° ESE) are right.
- **What should happen:** the map frames the two points 220 m apart.
- **Most likely at fault:** `MainActivity.fit` (`MainActivity.java:1096-1101`) takes `min`/`max` of the longitudes, so
  the box spans 359.998° the wrong way round. The other map fits (`LibreMap.fitNow`, the viewfinder's small maps) may
  have the same problem.
- **Test:** `dateLineMapShowsTheLine`.

### B4 (medium). Midnight sun: "never gets there" over a sky that is not shaded

- **Steps:** Build the shot with the subject at McMurdo (−77.849, 166.664) in November. Leave the sun where it starts
  (On top, about 8° up), On foot or Drone.
- **What happens:** the headline says "The sun never gets there here. Put the sun lower in the picture, or try another
  light." The sky from 0° to 35° is not shaded, so the picture says 8° is possible. Under the midnight sun the sun never
  comes down to 8°, so lower is impossible too: it has to go higher.
- **What should happen:** heights the body never passes in the days chosen are shaded, at the bottom as at the top, and
  the advice points the way that works.
- **Most likely at fault:** `Sky.possible` (`Sky.java:32-57`). It only checks `alt > top`, the day's highest (line 39).
  It never checks the day's lowest. With Drone every height under the top counts (line 41). On foot, a height with no
  sampled pass is allowed anyway (`out[a] = any || !found`, line 54). The advice is fixed text in
  `ComposeScreen.words()` (line 931).
- **Test:** `neverGetsThereAgreesWithTheShadingMcMurdo`.

### B5 (medium-low). Polar night: "Put the sun lower" when no height works

- **Steps:** Build the shot with the subject in Tromsø (69.648, 18.952) in late November.
- **What happens:** the whole sky is shaded (right: the sun barely rises), but the words say "Put the sun lower in the
  picture, or try another light". Neither helps in the next 6 weeks.
- **What should happen:** say that the sun does not rise high enough here in these days, and point to Look in: Next
  year.
- **Most likely at fault:** `ComposeScreen.words()` (line 931): the same words whatever the shading says.
- **Test:** `neverGetsThereAgreesWithTheShadingTromso`.

### B6 (medium-low). At sea the Distance note recommends a distance it never checked

- **Steps:** Build the shot with the subject in open water (the test uses the North Sea at 54.5, 3.0, with water-coloured
  map tiles), On foot. Open Settings.
- **What happens:** the headline correctly says "No spot on land for this picture" and Stand here is greyed. The
  Distance note says "Nothing works at 214 m. **Nearest that does: 3.1 km.**", and the slider is untinted (meaning "works")
  beyond 3 km. 3.1 km is open sea too: spots beyond 3 km are simply not looked at.
- **What should happen:** distances that were not checked are not offered as working. Here: "No distance works here:
  every spot is on water…".
- **Most likely at fault:** `ComposeScreen.workOutDist` (lines 623-660): `if (!fly && d <= DIST_CHECKED)` (line 641)
  leaves `land = true` for every distance beyond `DIST_CHECKED` (3000 m, line 611). `showRange` then names one of them
  (line 694).
- **Test:** `subjectOnWater`.

### B7 (low). While walking (Move) there is no key to stop walking

- **Steps:** Viewfinder → Move.
- **What happens:** the joystick and the small map take the lower part of the screen. The whole key row, Move
  included, is hidden, so nothing on screen ends walking; only the phone's Back does. The Move key's own close branch
  (`if (walkPanel == null) openWalk(); else closeWalk();`) can never be reached.
- **What should happen:** "What can I do next?" is answered on screen: a Done (or the Move key kept) on the walking
  panel.
- **Most likely at fault:** `FrameScreen.openWalk` (`FrameScreen.java:1276`, `panel.setVisibility(GONE)`).
- **Test:** `walkingHasAKeyToStop`.

### B8 (low). After a screen restart, Back from a shot opened in Saved goes to When, not to Saved

- **Steps:** Saved → open a shot (Your shot) → the screen restarts (the phone switches to dark mode at sunset) → Back.
- **What happens:** When (step 3 of 4). The Back table says: back to Saved.
- **Most likely at fault:** `MainActivity.fromSaved` (line 3125) is not written in `onSaveInstanceState` (line 263),
  so a restart forgets it.
- **Test:** `shotFromSavedBackAfterRestart`.

### B9 (low). A screen restart closes the viewfinder

- **Steps:** Your shot → Open the viewfinder → the screen restarts (dark mode switching; the manifest does not list
  `uiMode` in `configChanges`, so the activity is rebuilt).
- **What happens:** the viewfinder is gone; the app is on Your shot. Its time, the walk and the lens on screen are
  lost (the lens and height are saved).
- **What should happen:** the same screen comes back, as it does for the steps.
- **Most likely at fault:** `MainActivity.onSaveInstanceState` (line 263) saves only `in_tool` and `settings`, not
  that the viewfinder (or Saved, or search) was open. Also `AndroidManifest.xml:23`.
- **Test:** `restartInTheViewfinderKeepsIt`.

### B10 (low). A guide opened from Settings closes to Home, not to Settings

- **Steps:** Home → Settings → Guides → Build the shot → Back (or Skip).
- **What happens:** Home. The Back table says a guide closes "nothing changed", but Settings has been closed.
- **Most likely at fault:** `MainActivity.java:2481` and `2485` (`onHelp`, `onGuide`) call `closeSettings()` before
  showing the guide. It looks deliberate; the owner may prefer to keep Settings under the guide.
- **Test:** `guideFromSettingsBackReturnsToSettings`.

## Noted, not counted as bugs

- **You on the subject.** Line up a street refuses a point within 10 m of the other ("That is on top of the other
  point. Pick a place at least 10 m away."), from the map and from typed coordinates. A saved line or a shared link can
  still carry it, since `ShotLink.decode` does not check. With You 0 m from the subject, When says "Never lines up here.
  The sun is never behind the subject from here. It is behind you Today" and offers Line it up and Show Behind you,
  which mean nothing at 0 m. No crash.
- **North Pole.** The flat map cannot show above about 85° N, so the pins are drawn at the map's top edge, 1,384 km
  wide. The answers (polar night: "Soonest Friday, Mar 19") are sensible. No crash.
- **Map labels overlap.** On Your shot the body's label ("Moon 3:40 AM") can sit over the subject's ("Big Ben") when
  the line leaves the map near the subject. Cosmetic.
- **Pictures taken too early.** A picture taken right after Your shot shows, before the sheet settles, can have the
  pins off the map. Once the sheet settles both pins are in view (`yourShotMapShowsBothPins*`). Not a bug.

## What the sweep covers and found sound

- **Back at every step** of both tools, in Your shot, the viewfinder (height dialog, joystick, its guide), Saved,
  Settings and its dropdowns, the three guides, search, the map look sheet and the Sun / Moon list. Back never left
  the app before Home, and Home always left. No dead end found.
- **Double taps:** Next (one step), See it in the viewfinder (one viewfinder, one Back closes it), Save on Your shot and
  in the viewfinder (one shot), Done, Fly here (one step), Line it up (Undo returns to the first spot), Show Behind
  you, and the bell.
- **Restarts and process death** at every step, with Settings, Saved, search and the joystick open: no crash, the step
  is kept (the viewfinder and Saved's origin are lost: B8, B9).
- **No network** (every connection refused while the phone says it is online; and the phone offline): both tools,
  saving ("Saved as …" at once), the viewfinder, buildings ("could not be fetched"), heights, and search ("No answer
  from the map search. Check the connection…").
- **Odd places:**
  - Tromsø, McMurdo and the North Pole: polar night and midnight sun, through both tools and both bodies.
  - Across the date line (bearing and times right; B3 is the map).
  - 5 m, 0 m and 30 km apart, and the subject at sea.
  - No crash anywhere, and When always offers a way on or says why not.
- **Moon mode** through both tools. Every Light × Look in (× How full for the moon) in Build the shot (a sun and a moon
  run of 15 and 27 combinations), with Any light naming its side. The Distance slider at both ends. The lens at both
  ends in Build the shot and the viewfinder.
- **Line up a street:** Behind subject / Behind you toggled four times while working (the card agrees with the switch).
  Line it up, Undo, Line it up again (the same move). Swap twice, Clear then Undo, Clear then Back. The Day chart a
  year forward and back.
- **Saved:** save, rename (an empty name keeps the old one), the bell on and off, open (Your shot knows its new name),
  remove with Undo (the reminder comes back too), the Trip tab's lit bell, and trip days at both ends.
- **Shared links:** a link holding nothing ("That link does not hold a shot SunWhere can open."), a good link opened
  twice, and a link with You on its subject. A link with no time hangs (B2).
- **The crash log:** every sweep test fails if `last-crash.txt` has an entry, so a failure the app caught
  (`CrashLog.caught`) or a background thread that died would show. None did.
