SunLighn, redesigned

Two directions drawn from your Braun references, on six real screens each, with the app's flow, words, amber sun, violet moon and both themes kept. Switch the body and the theme to see each screen change.

APocket map

The app as a printed city map: pale paper, map greys, a park green and a water blue, with the line drawn in the body's colour like the route on a folded map. Every step's sheet ends in a map key that is the answer itself.

Palette
Paper Ink Sun, the line Moon You Can't
Signature
The map key: a ruled block under each step listing You, Subject, the Line and the body, each with its swatch from the map. Home is a booklet cover with the body as a quarter disc in the corner (a crescent for the moon).
At night
The night edition: navy paper, the map inverted to navy, the same key.
Risk
Quiet. The answer reads as a map label more than a headline, so the time sits smaller than in B.

BReadout

The app as a handheld navigator: every step answers in one large line, like "300 m, turn right", followed by a strip of three numbers. Grey casework, a graphite readout panel, and round keys in the body's colour for the one thing to do next.

Palette
Case Readout Sun, main key Moon You Can't
Signature
The readout: a graphite panel with the instruction in large figures ("Stand 5.7 km back", "4:54 PM") and a three-cell strip under it (when, how high, which lens), split by hairlines. The main action is a round key.
At night
The case goes black and the readout stays graphite, so the numbers read the same by day and by night.
Risk
Louder. The dark panel is heavy on a light screen, and the map gets less room than in A.

Both keep

  • Home, the two tools, their steps, Your shot and Back, exactly as now.
  • Every word in the app's vocabulary (Subject, Lined up, Your shot, Stand here...).
  • Amber for the sun and violet for the moon, following the body.
  • Light and dark themes; the viewfinder stays dark.
  • IBM Plex Sans Arabic, so Arabic stays possible.
  • One shape per meaning: dropdowns, the segmented track, tabs, no pills.

How to choose

Reply with A or B. You can also mix them, for example "A, with B's readout on Your shot". The one you pick gets built into the app across every screen, not only these six.

Times, distances and places are examples for Riyadh on Nov 17, 2026, drawn to show the layout; the app works them out.
