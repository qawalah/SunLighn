# People Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Handles, profiles with stats, follow and friends, blocking, likes on guides and photos, and admin stats on the server (0.2), then a People screen in the app (3.37).

**Architecture:** The existing Javalin + SQLite server gains tables and routes in the same process (new classes `People`, `Likes`, `Admin` beside `App`/`Store`). The app's `Social` gets the calls and a small cache; a new `PeopleScreen` is opened from a Home key. Offline-first stays: nothing outside People needs the server.

**Tech Stack:** Java 21, Javalin 6, sqlite-jdbc, JUnit 5 (server); Android framework, org.json, Robolectric (app).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-people-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Never change `applicationId` (`dev.qawalah.sunlighn`); app release: versionCode 79 -> 80, versionName "3.36.0" -> "3.37.0".
- Handle: lowercase `[a-z0-9_]{3,20}`, unique, changeable once in 30 days; reserved: `admin`, `api`, `g`, `c`, `u`, `me`, `sunwhere`, `support`.
- Bio at most 160 characters. Levels: `link`, `public`, `friends`. Photo like targets: 0 (the guide) or 1 to 3.
- Words in the app: Handle, Profile, Follow, Friends, Like, Block (one word per idea). No pills (dropdowns), Back closes People to Home, every key says what it does.
- Existing 3.36 phones keep working: `PUT /api/guides/{id}` still accepts `listed` (true -> public, false -> link).
- App stays whole offline; People says "Needs a network" and shows its last answer marked "Not updated".
- Server build is independent (`server/`, `../gradlew -p server test`); never touch `settings.gradle` of the app.

## Review Focus

- A blocked person's profile, guides, likes and requests are 404/refused both ways, and blocking removes follow and friendship (`PeopleTest.blockCutsBothWays`).
- A friends-level guide is 404 for strangers and followers, visible to friends and its owner, and absent from city pages, sitemap and feeds (`LevelsTest.friendsGuideIsPrivateToFriends`).
- Handles: taken, reserved, bad characters, and a second change inside 30 days are refused with a reason (`ProfileTest.handleRules`).
- A like on your own guide, on a guide you cannot see, or twice, changes nothing wrong (`LikesTest.likeRules`).
- A banned account's token stops working and its guides vanish; unban brings them back (`AdminTest.banAndUnban`).
- Migration of a 0.1 database: handles made unique for names that collide or are not Latin (`ProfileTest.migrationMakesUniqueHandles`).
