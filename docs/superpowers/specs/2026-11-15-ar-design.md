# SunLighn 3.5: AR in the viewfinder, with a landmark to set the compass

Date: 2026-11-15. Owner: "an AR mode ... see sun or moon movement"; asked whether positions must be calibrated;
then "implement it", and the compass is to be set from **any landmark**, not necessarily the subject.

## Why

The drawn viewfinder shows the shot; on location the photographer wants the sun's or moon's path over the real
view. Positions are not the problem (a GPS fix is plenty: the sun's direction does not change over kilometres); the
phone's compass is (often 5 to 20 degrees off). A landmark of known position fixes it.

## Design (Claude's calls; owner approved the shape on 2026-11-15)

- **Where:** the viewfinder's Compass key. With Compass on, the live back camera shows behind the scene: the
  shot's body, its path with the hours, "Lined up", the compass strip and the heights. No drawn sky, land, stars or
  dimmed edges in AR; the subject is a marker (the camera shows the real one). Camera permission is asked at the
  first Compass press; refused, Compass works as before over the drawn sky.
- **Lens:** while the camera shows, the picture takes the camera's own field of view (its focal length and sensor
  size, and the part of the picture the screen shows), portrait; the user's lens comes back when Compass is off.
- **Calibrate the compass:** a key under the scene while Compass is on. The lower part of the screen becomes a small
  map: "Tap a landmark you can see: a tower, a mast, a hilltop." The subject is offered first, but any point will
  do. Then "Point the cross at it, then Set." A cross is drawn in the middle of the picture. Set: the correction is
  the true bearing from you to the landmark less the compass reading; it is kept (prefs) and said ("Compass set:
  7° to the right"). "Reset" removes it.
- **Height** of the subject: not needed in AR (the camera sees it); kept as it is for the lined-up moments.

## Not in this release

Pitch calibration (tilt is usually good), a camera picture saved with the shot, AR outside the viewfinder.

## Tests

`FrameMath.headingOffset` (unit); the calibration flow (a rotation sensor added in Robolectric, a reading fed by
hand, a landmark picked, Set: the view then aims at the landmark's true bearing); camera allowed shows the camera
view and AR drawing (no sky drawn); camera refused keeps the drawn sky.
