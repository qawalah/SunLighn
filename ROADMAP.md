# SunLighn roadmap

What is planned or proposed, newest thinking first. Done work moves to the decisions in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Proposed: two tools instead of three (owner's question, 2026-11-15; waiting for a yes)

Where to stand and Build the shot answer the same question ("where do I stand, and when?") from a subject: one
by a form, one by the picture. Since 3.3 the form has the same picture on top, so they are one tool shown twice.

1. **Line up a street**: you already know where you stand. Unchanged.
2. **Find the shot** (or keep the name Build the shot: the owner's choice): you know what you want in the picture.
   Build the shot with the best of Where to stand in it:
   - The picture is the main control: drag the sun or moon, frame (swing and tilt), pinch the subject, the lens.
   - Where the sun sits becomes three quick keys that snap the body there: On the horizon, Behind the middle,
     On top.
   - Distance, Lens, Light (with Any and golden hour), On foot / Drone, How full (moon) in the folded Settings row.
   - The days found in the When chooser, grouped by Sunset and Sunrise; Stand here beside it.
   - Ends on Your shot, as every tool.

Lost: typing an exact distance before seeing the picture (the slider stays in Settings). Gained: one tool fewer to
explain, a simpler Home, no overlap. To decide: the name; whether Where to stand's spot list (several days, each
its own spot on the map) is kept as a view of When.

When done: Home, the tutorial (three tool cards become two), `Flow` (the STAND tool goes; Continue of a saved
STAND flow opens the new tool), the Words section, the three-questions table, ScreensTest, one Ali run.

## Open from earlier audits and reviews

- Arabic and right-to-left (in the design language, not done).
- Turning the map with two fingers; weather; units and reminder lead time in Settings.
- Trip lists a moment once per saved shot, so two shots on one line list it twice.
- The Terrain map is hard to read at night.
- Line up a street's "lined up" moments do not yet hide a sun below the subject's ground.
- Compass and AR (the camera, the calibration) need testing on a real phone.
- "When" in Build the shot can wrap onto two lines ("Tomorrow to / Nov 17").
